How most people ask
summarise this MDA data

Here is a general overview of your dataset. It contains treatment coverage figures across several districts and years. Overall, coverage appears to vary. You may wish to review the districts individually to identify any patterns of interest…

How AMPLIFY teaches you to ask
TaskSummarise treatment coverage for the 2025 MDA round.
Context42 districts, three years of data. Some districts are missing round two.
PersonaYou are a data officer briefing the national NTD programme manager.
FormatOne paragraph, then a table of every district below 65% — flag the gaps rather than filling them.

Same data. Same free tool. The difference is the ask — and unlike the tool, the ask is something you can teach, write down, and hand to a colleague.

The design constraint

Nothing in this course costs money to use.

That is a deliberate limit, not a compromise. AMPLIFY is built on the free tiers of general-purpose AI tools, so a programme officer in Juba and one in Accra start from the same place — and so nothing stops working when a grant closes.

No subscriptions
Free tiers only. Every exercise is designed to fit inside their limits, including the ones on message length and daily usage.
No procurement
Nothing to license, approve, or renew through a ministry finance office. A participant can start the day they finish the session.
No lock-in
The course teaches a method — stacking several tools against one problem and checking their work against each other — rather than a product. When a tool changes, the method holds.
Low bandwidth
Materials are built to be usable on the connections participants actually have, and to be re-run offline where the work allows it.

For funders

What a pilot this size is actually for.

Sixteen people is not a scale claim. It is a test of one question: can national NTD programmes build durable analytical capability using tools that cost nothing — and does that capability survive contact with a real ministry workload?

What exists now
Seven modules of bilingual course material — slides, worksheets, and exercises — reusable by any programme without further development cost.
Where it sits
A trained AI focal point inside seven national NTD programmes, working with their own live data rather than a training dataset.
What is transferable
A documented method that does not depend on any one vendor, and can be taught by instructors who are not AI specialists.
