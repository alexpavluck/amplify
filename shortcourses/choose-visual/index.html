      Chart rules for ESPEN data

      • One y-axis per chart. Put coverage (%) and people treated (counts) on separate charts.
      • Start percentage axes at 0 and draw the WHO threshold as a reference line.
      • Dash or flag the latest year if it looks incompletely reported.
      • Use shades of one colour for ordered categories like endemicity; a different colour only when categories are unrelated.
      • Label lines directly and always give a legend; never rely on colour alone.
      • Put the table and years under every chart.
